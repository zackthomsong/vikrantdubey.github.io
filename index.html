About the researcher

I research how libraries meet emerging technology — from blockchain-secured lending to AI-assisted literacy — and how institutions measure up on the web.

Dubey researches emerging technologies, knowledge management, sentiment analysis and bibliometrics, with a recurring focus on how Indian academic libraries perform online and how new tools — blockchain, makerspaces, MOOCs — reshape library practice.

Current work sits at the Department of Library & Information Science, Babasaheb Bhimrao Ambedkar University, Lucknow, a central university. Prior to this, Dubey served as Assistant Professor in the Department of Library and Information Science at Mahanand Mission Harijan College, Ghaziabad.

  • Digital library performance & accessibility auditing
  • Blockchain & NFTs in library management
  • Information literacy standards & frameworks
  • Digital preservation strategy
  • Sentiment analysis & data analytics
  • Makerspaces & emerging library technology
